# Surface brief: BAYOU WELLNESS home and storefront

Scope: `src/templates/home.mjs` plus the shared header, footer and cards. Visitor mode: Persuade (home), Operate-lite for cart, catalog and map.

Audience and job: phone-first shoppers in Kazakhstan (kk/ru first, en/ko secondary) choosing hair and scalp care and asking a manager on WhatsApp; salon and shop owners deciding to apply as partners; people locating an official partner. Proof on hand: seven AROMATICA Rosemary products legible on packaging, the glossy logo, the supplied photography, a verified-partner rule. No prices, partner records, ingredient documents, testimonials or counts exist and none are invented.

Constraints: brief-pinned world (logo, spec palette, Montserrat headings, Manrope body with Onest for Kazakh), four languages equal, no horizontal scroll or stretch on phones, fixed header with hamburger top right and language switcher beside it, WhatsApp float bottom right with yellow and green waves, no medical claims, static site (no server).

Unresolved: real prices and stock, real partner list, official ingredient and usage documents, delivery and return terms, production domain.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A botanical clean lab. The glossy red-and-green logo is the only loud object; everything else is a mineral-white ground lit by real product photography. It refuses the centered stock beauty hero, equal icon-card feature rows, blob gradients and fake social proof.

OWN-WORLD: Page ground #F7F9F6 with sage tiles (#E8F3DB to #D3E8BE) into which white-background pack shots multiply; deep forest bands (#153A08, derived from Deep Green) for the map teaser, B2B and footer; glossy lime CTA with ink text (never white on lime); red only on sale and alert; Montserrat 800 at -0.035em for headings; arch frames only in the hero; pills for controls, 20px for cards. Recognisable with the copy removed by the multiplied bottle on the sage arch and the lime glossy buttons.

STORY: Within seconds the visitor sees real rosemary scalp and hair products, understands BAYOU is a verified retailer with a partner network, and either opens the catalog, asks on WhatsApp, finds a partner or applies as a business.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left, H1 (three lines on desktop, four on phone), lead, primary "browse products" lime pill, secondary "partner map" outline pill, tertiary "become a partner" text link. Right, the Root Enhancer photo multiplied into a sage arch, a round crop of the PDRN tubes overlapping bottom-left, a small group photo top-right, and a white chip linking to the product. On a 375px phone: header (logo, cart, language, hamburger), H1, lead, stacked CTAs, then the stage; WhatsApp button fixed bottom right.

FORM: brief-pinned (no catalog form rolled); seed: none (brief-pinned, concept-seed not run).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
